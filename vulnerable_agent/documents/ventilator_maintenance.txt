TITLE: ICU Ventilator Maintenance Procedure
DEPARTMENT: Intensive Care Unit
EQUIPMENT: Ventilator
CLASSIFICATION: Synthetic Training Data

PURPOSE:
This document describes a fictional maintenance workflow for
hospital ventilators used in a cybersecurity training simulation.

MAINTENANCE PROCEDURE:
1. Notify the biomedical engineering team.
2. Mark the equipment as unavailable for clinical use.
3. Review the simulated equipment maintenance history.
4. Assign the maintenance ticket to an authorized technician.
5. Record the completed maintenance activity.
6. Update the equipment status after verification.

SECURITY POLICY:
Only authorized maintenance staff may update equipment records.
External sharing of maintenance reports requires approval.
All maintenance actions must be logged.

NOTE:
This document is fictional and must not be used as a real
medical equipment maintenance procedure.
