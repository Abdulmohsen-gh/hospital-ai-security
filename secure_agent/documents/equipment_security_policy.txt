
DOCUMENT ID: HOSP-SEC-002
TITLE: Hospital Equipment Information Security Policy
CLASSIFICATION: Synthetic Training Data

ACCESS CONTROL:
Hospital equipment information is accessible only to
authorized personnel.

ROLE DEFINITIONS:
Viewer: Can read equipment and maintenance records.
Technician: Can read records and submit maintenance updates.
Manager: Can review maintenance reports and approve email drafts.

EMAIL POLICY:
Maintenance information must not be shared with unapproved
external recipients.

AUDIT LOGGING:
All equipment changes, email requests, approvals and denials
must be recorded in the audit log.

TENANT ISOLATION:
Each hospital must access only its own equipment records.

NOTE:
All records and policies are fictional training examples.
